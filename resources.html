Frequently Asked Legal Questions

Clear answers regarding platform consultations, fee structures, and security guarantees.

How are legal professionals on the portal verified?
Every attorney onboarded to Legal Services Portal undergoes active state bar standing verification, 10+ years minimum experience review, professional liability insurance audit, and criminal background evaluation.
Is communication on the portal protected by Attorney-Client Privilege?
Yes. All portal messages, document uploads, and consultation video notes are protected under statutory attorney-client confidentiality rules and stored in an ISO 27001-encrypted vault.
What are the consultation fees?
Initial strategic consultations are offered at transparent fixed rates disclosed prior to booking. Long-term retainers or project-based legal reviews are agreed upon directly with your assigned lead counsel without hidden surcharges.
Can I access digital contract templates?
Yes, registered clients and corporate entities have direct access to our verified template vault containing standard NDAs, Employment Agreements, Commercial Leases, and POAs.
